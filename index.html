StudyMate AI

Intelligent Student Study Assistant

StudyMate AI helps students organise their studies, practice what they've learned, and track progress — all without needing an internet connection or any external services.

Our Mission

Students often struggle to structure their revision time effectively, find relevant practice questions, and maintain a clear view of their study progress. StudyMate AI addresses these challenges by providing a single, distraction-free environment where every study tool is one click away — no logins, no subscriptions, no internet required.

How It Works

1️⃣

Plan Your Study

Enter your subjects, topics, available hours, and target date. The planner generates a personalised schedule.

2️⃣

Practice with Questions

Select a subject and difficulty. Get practice questions with hidden answers — reveal them when you're ready to check.

3️⃣

Track Your Progress

Add study tasks to your checklist, mark them complete, and watch your progress bar grow.

Core Features

🗓️

Smart Study Planner

Distributes your study hours proportionally across subjects and generates a day-by-day calendar.

❓

Practice Question Engine

288 curated questions across 6 subjects and 3 difficulty tiers. Template fallback for any topic.

📊

Progress Tracker

Visual progress bar and task checklist. Filter by status, mark tasks done, and clear completed items.

💾

Offline & Private

All data stays in your browser's localStorage. No server, no login, no data leaves your device.

📱

Fully Responsive

Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Clean layout that adapts to any screen size.

⚡

Zero Setup

Open index.html in any modern browser. No installation, no build tools, no internet needed.

Who Is This For?

StudyMate AI is designed for secondary school and university students who want a simple, focused tool to help them structure revision, test their knowledge, and stay on top of their study workload — without the distraction of complex apps or the cost of subscription services.

Architecture Overview

StudyMate AI follows a simple three-layer architecture:

  • Presentation Layer — index.html + CSS files: all markup and styling. Sections are toggled by the router.
  • Application Logic Layer — JS modules (app.js, planner.js, questions.js, progress.js, dashboard.js, ui.js): feature logic, DOM rendering.
  • Data Layer — storage.js + data/questionBank.js: localStorage persistence and static question data.

How to Run

  1. Download or clone the studymate-ai/ folder.
  2. Open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
  3. No installation, no build step, no internet connection required.
  4. All data is saved automatically in your browser's localStorage.
